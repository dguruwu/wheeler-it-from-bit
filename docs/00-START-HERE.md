# START HERE — what this repository is

A third-party collection (no affiliation with the late John Archibald Wheeler's estate)
working on one question Wheeler left open in his 1990 essay *Information, Physics, Quantum:
The Search for Links* (Zurek ed., *Complexity, Entropy and the Physics of Information*):

> "It from bit. Otherwise put, every it — every particle, every field of force, even the
> space-time continuum itself — derives its function, its meaning, its very existence
> entirely... from the apparatus-elicited answers to yes-or-no questions, binary choices,
> bits... all things physical are information-theoretic in origin and this is a
> participatory universe."

Wheeler said the IT comes from the BIT. He never closed the loop on where the BIT comes
from. Our proposed completion (speculative, tagged [EXTENSION] wherever it appears):

> **I = E + O.** Information = Energy + Operation/Observation.
> The bit is manufactured, in the act, by an observer running an operation on energy.
> It-from-bit becomes: it-from-I; I-from-E-plus-O.

Consequences developed in docs/: the participator's merge (an observer cannot render a
world without being rendered inside it — the headset always renders the head wearing it),
and the reading of Wheeler's "posing of yes-no questions" as the operation that selects.
